01 About

I'm a developer and designer who likes the unglamorous middle of the problem — the part where an idea has to survive contact with real users, real data, and real deadlines.

Over the last few years I've worked across the stack: shipping front-ends that feel fast, designing APIs that are pleasant to consume, and untangling the kind of legacy code that everyone else steps around. I care about clarity over cleverness, and about writing things down so the next person doesn't have to reverse-engineer my reasoning.

Outside of work you'll find me reading long-form non-fiction, taking photographs I never get around to editing, and slowly learning to cook something other than pasta.
