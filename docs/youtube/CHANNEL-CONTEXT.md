## Monetization

- **YPP targets:** 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours (or 10M Shorts views)
- **Early access tier:** 500 subs + 3,000 watch hours (fan funding only)
- **Realistic timeline:** 15–18 months from Sept 2026, i.e. late 2027, and only
  if weekly uploads stay unbroken. See METRICS-LOG for the working.
- **Revenue, honestly:** Malayalam finance RPM ≈ $0.8–1.5 / 1,000 views.
  Two-year cumulative ad revenue estimate: AED 300–1,600 total. The real value
  is the personal brand — inbound consulting, paid courses, a Telegram
  community, sponsorships.
- **UAE licensing:** creators earning revenue in the UAE need a trade licence
  + UAE Media Council permit (AED 5,000–15,000/year); financial content may
  also need SCA clearance. Non-monetised hobbyists are exempt — currently
  exempt, and only worth licensing once earnings are meaningful.

## What's working

- **Search traffic.** EA Course 43.8% search, US CPA 41% search. Both are
  permanently ranked and carry 25 of 41 total watch hours between them.
- **Low-competition niche.** Malayalam finance certification content is
  genuinely underserved.
- **Question-format thumbnails.** "Coaching Really Needed?" → 25.9% CTR vs ~6%
  for plain informational thumbnails.
- **Content clusters.** Overview video + FAQ follow-up. The CPA pair worked best.
- **Longer videos.** CFA at 11:04 produces ~2x the watch time per view of a
  6-minute video.
- **Retention improves on its own** as YouTube refines audience matching —
  EA Course went 23.8% → 38% without any re-edit.

## Known weaknesses

- **Intro hook.** Biggest drop-off is 0:30–1:30 across every video. The trigger
  is the face disappearing when the cut goes to full-screen slides.
- **Inconsistent uploads.** Two Sundays missed. The channel coasts on existing
  momentum, but new uploads drive the spikes — and compounding is the entire
  monetisation thesis.
- **Subscriber conversion.** WhatsApp-driven viewers rarely subscribe;
  search-driven viewers convert better.
- **DipIFR** is the weakest video (~30% retention, weaker hook than the rest).

## Standing decisions

- Stay strictly in niche: international accounting/finance certifications for
  Malayalam speakers. No personal finance, hospital administration, or other
  unrelated fields until 10K+ subscribers.
- Off-topic viewer questions get a polite redirect, not a video.
- No artificial view inflation (looped playback etc.) — violates YouTube ToS
  and doesn't count toward watch hours anyway.
- Telegram community planned, not launched. Suggested by viewer @VishalAsok.
- Instagram is a small but new external traffic source (~15% of external views).

## Working preferences

When a YouTube Studio screenshot is shared:

1. Append a row to the tracker in `METRICS-LOG.md` (day, subs, views, watch hours)
2. Analyse what changed and why
3. Assess honestly — do not inflate small numbers into wins
4. Give exactly one concrete action item

For a new video topic:

- Thorough web research: fees, salary (India + UAE), eligibility, exam
  structure, job market
- SEO-optimised title options (Malayalam + English mix)
- Description with timestamps and keywords
- Thumbnail concept (question format preferred)
- A strong opening hook, written in Malayalam

Be direct about problems. "Retention is your bottleneck" beats encouragement.
